What is Qerrastar?

Qerrastar is an AI-powered assistant for students that helps improve learning efficiency through smart digital tools. It allows students to generate quizzes, practice previous year questions (PYQ), summarize long text instantly, and convert files using built-in PDF to JPG and JPG to PDF converters.

Designed for modern learners, Qerrastar combines study tools, AI-based summarization, and quiz generation into one powerful smart learning platform. Whether you want to prepare for exams, revise concepts, or quickly convert documents, Qerrastar simplifies your workflow.

Key Features of Qerrastar

  • AI Quiz Generator for fast practice
  • Previous Year Question (PYQ) practice system
  • Free PDF to JPG converter tool
  • JPG to PDF conversion tool
  • AI Text Summarizer to shorten long content

Qerrastar is built to support students who want faster learning, smarter revision, and efficient digital study tools in one place.
